### ASO strategy notes (EN markets)

- `fitness` (volume 10/10, very high competition) — broad
- `nutrition` (10/10, high) — combine with coach
- `calories` (10/10, high) — MFP-curious users
- `macros` (8/10, medium) — expert term, high conversion
- `workout` (10/10, high) — Strong/Hevy alt
- `coach` (8/10, high) — differentiator
- `strength` (8/10, medium) — specific
- `muscle` (9/10, high) — combines
- `bodybuilding` (7/10, medium) — niche but high intent
- `gym` (10/10, very high) — broad
- `1rm` (5/10, low competition) — expert sweet spot
- `AI` (9/10, growing fast) — differentiator

### Keywords to AVOID

❌ `MyFitnessPal`, `Strong`, `Hevy`, `Fitbod` — competitor trademark, Apple rejection
❌ `lose weight fast`, `guaranteed`, `miracle` — Apple flags health claims
❌ `free` — ignored by Apple algorithm
❌ Medical conditions (`diabetes`, `obesity`) — review risk
